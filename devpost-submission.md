# Title

IsoPact

## One-line Summary

Outcome settlement infrastructure that keeps independent enterprise agents from succeeding locally while the business fails globally.

## Problem

A $200 missing-order case can project $650 of compensation when independent agents each succeed locally.

## Solution

An Outcome Pact combines semantic reservation, deterministic authorization, authoritative evidence, constrained reconciliation, and signed receipts beneath the fleet.

## Why This Matters

Commerce operations supervisors—not AI developers—must answer whether the business settled correctly after every agent reported completion.

## How We Used AI

Gemini 3.5 Flash compiles intent and reasons about constrained recovery. It cannot authorize refunds or invent compensation; deterministic code owns authority.

## How We Used Codex

Codex assisted implementation, testing, deployment automation, adversarial auditing, and documentation. Architecture, direction, acceptance criteria, and submission responsibility remain the entrant's work.

## Key Features

Four specialized Runtime agents; semantic duplicate prevention; exclusive resolution slots; ranked evidence; `OUTCOME_UNKNOWN`; TOCTOU-safe reconciliation; KMS receipts; causal observability; frozen adversarial benchmark.
